Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Alvidore Options Tracker ("the app") is a private, single-user tool operated by its owner for personal options portfolio tracking. It is not offered to the public.

Information accessed

The app uses Google sign-in to confirm the owner's identity (email address only) and uses the Gmail "send" permission to email the owner's own review reports from the owner's own Gmail account. The app cannot read, list, or delete any email.

Data storage

Portfolio data is stored in a private database accessible only to the owner. Google authorization tokens are stored in private, encrypted secret stores.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above.
